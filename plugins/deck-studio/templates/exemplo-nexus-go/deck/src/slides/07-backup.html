Hyperparameters

Optimization settings of the final second-generation campaign (Table A.2). Values marked varies are part of the search space.

Late fusion: grid and selected coefficients

71 configurations per ontology, chosen on validation. Both conditional rules converged on the flattest sigmoid the grid offers.

Early fusion: provenance × source

Provenance is the head with which the consumed GNN embedding was trained; source is the head whose depth and retention were used for the fixed branch. Validation set.

Isolated vs connected proteins

wFmax on the test set. Every ProtT5 route scores worse on connected proteins; the First Generation is the only route that improves with connectivity.

Degree and per-protein F1

Rank correlation between degree and per-protein F1, test set. All sequence-aware routes have small negative correlations; the graph routes make them less negative.

Baselines: adaptations, cost and coverage

Hyperparameters were taken from each method's reference configuration; changes are hardware constraints, not a search. Coverage is the fraction of test proteins that receive a prediction.

Hierarchy closure uses is_a only

  • Ancestors reachable only through part_of do not receive their descendants' scores
  • Affects 387 of 3,507 ancestor-descendant pairs in BP (11.0%), 2,056 of 4,405 in CC (46.7%), a single pair in MF
  • Applied identically to every method; the ground truth closes over is_a and part_of

This follows the DeepGO evaluation convention. The direction of its effect on the metrics is not guaranteed, and it matters most for Cellular Component.
