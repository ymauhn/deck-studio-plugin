NEXUS-GO

Network-Extended Unified Sequence Embeddings for Gene Ontology Function Annotation

Yeonatan Mauhnoom

Advisor: Prof. Dr. Zanoni Dias · Co-advisors: Prof. Dr. Hélio Pedrini and Dr. Gabriel Bianchin de Oliveira

Master's dissertation defense · Institute of Computing, University of Campinas (Unicamp) · November 16, 2026

Good morning. My dissertation asks how far a protein interaction network can go toward predicting Gene Ontology annotations, alone and combined with a protein language model.

The answer is NEXUS-GO, a framework that propagates ProtT5 embeddings over STRING.
